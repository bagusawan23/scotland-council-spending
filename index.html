      Data. Local Government Benchmarking Framework (LGBF), published by the Improvement Service and distributed through Spatial Hub Scotland under the UK Open Government Licence. Real values are the inflation-adjusted series in the source file, expressed in 2024-25 prices. Years are financial years: 2023-24 runs from April 2023 to March 2024.

      Boundaries. Local Authority Boundaries for Scotland from Spatial Hub Scotland, simplified for the web; islands under half a square kilometre are left out.

      Reading the costs. A higher cost per unit does not always mean a council is less efficient. Rural and island delivery, the mix of people served and the way a service is provided all change what a unit costs. Scotland figures are the national values published in the LGBF.
